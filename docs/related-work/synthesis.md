# Literature-to-Implementation Synthesis

**Evidence checkpoint: 9 November 2026.** The [index](README.md) contains 18 individually reviewed papers. This is a selective collection, not an exhaustive or systematic review. Each linked note records source access, findings, limitations, and a proposed experiment.

The current [computational milestone](../../reports/milestone-2026-11-09/README.md) implements a narrow, interpretable allocation model. The literature informs its questions and boundaries; the benchmark does not reproduce all referenced methods.

## Testable conclusions from the milestone

The implemented optimizer makes cost, service, and stability trade-offs inspectable. Its numerical validation and synthetic comparisons establish a reproducible computational baseline. They do not establish a new optimization algorithm, causal visitor effects, or superior field performance.

The next empirical step is to obtain appropriately usable demand and capacity observations, define meaningful service and fairness requirements, and evaluate chronological forecast-to-decision pipelines. Sensing uncertainty, pricing response, recommendation adherence, and grounded explanation should then be added as independently testable components. These are proposed extensions, not completed achievements.
